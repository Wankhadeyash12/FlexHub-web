# FlexHub

FlexHub is an event management platform for colleges, communities, and student organizations.
Organizers can publish events and manage registrations, while participants can discover events,
form teams, register, and track their participation.

## Live Application

Visit FlexHub: **https://flexhub-web-2.onrender.com**

## What You Can Do

### Organizers

- Create and publish events
- Add event descriptions, rules, schedules, venues, and registration fees
- Set minimum and maximum team sizes
- Upload event banners
- Review, approve, or reject registrations
- Track registrations, approvals, and revenue through the dashboard

### Participants

- Browse published events
- View event details and guidelines
- Register individually or with a team
- Complete event payments when required
- Track registration and approval status

## How to Use FlexHub

### As an Organizer

1. Open the live application.
2. Select **Register** and choose **Organizer**.
3. Sign in to open the organizer dashboard.
4. Create an event and add its details.
5. Publish the event when it is ready.
6. Use the dashboard to manage registrations and view analytics.

### As a Participant

1. Open the live application.
2. Select **Register** and choose **Participant**.
3. Sign in and browse the available events.
4. Open an event to review its details and rules.
5. Register individually or add your team members.
6. Complete payment when required and track your registration from the dashboard.

## Project Highlights

- Separate organizer and participant experiences
- Support for online, offline, and hybrid events
- Individual and team registration workflows
- Registration approval management
